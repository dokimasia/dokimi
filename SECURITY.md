# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report a vulnerability to security@dokimi.dev, or through a private security advisory of
[the repository](https://github.com/dokimasia/dokimi/security/advisories/new). Do not open a public
issue.

A report states the affected version, the steps that reproduce the vulnerability, and its impact.
